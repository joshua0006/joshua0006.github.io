Arrow Escape Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Android app com.joshuaapp.arrowescape

This policy explains what information the mobile game Arrow Escape ("the app", "we") and the services it uses collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers and do not receive your personal information.

Game data stored on your device. Your level progress, coins, unlocked cosmetics, settings (sound, haptics, accessibility) and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic data (ads viewed or tapped, crashes and performance)

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the main menu.

Personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of whether you own the purchase, so it can turn off interstitial ads and restore the purchase if you reinstall. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and connect to Google Play Billing
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings)
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above
  • Billing: to make and restore the Remove Ads purchase

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. You can request deletion of Google advertising data through your Google account, or contact us and we will help where we can.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
